## P1: the numbers can't yet be believed

### 5. PASS has stopped meaning much
42 of 53 ideas have a PASS run (44 of 63 runs). Two days earlier it was 1 of 9. [V] The reasons:
- The bar rises only within one idea, so a new idea resets to p < 0.05.
- The unseen years aren't unseen by the person choosing. MU, TXN, TSM and NVDA were picked knowing how they did.
- All five Book 2 ideas are overlapping chip bets at p = 0.024-0.048, not independent evidence.
- A buy-and-hold claim only asks whether the stock beat SPY over the last 30% of the data.
- Walk-forward folds are computed but don't affect the verdict (`verdict_of(on_u[0], p, bar)`).
- The screen-to-idea path selects on today's data and then backtests the past. A grep finds no warning for this. [I]

Do:
- Keep a hub-wide test count and adjust for it (deflated Sharpe or false-discovery control). Show "Nth idea tested" on each verdict.
- Group related ideas into one family.
- Label pick-then-test ideas "hindsight-selected".
- Make walk-forward part of PASS.

### 6. Option backtests skipped the calibration
The options design required a skew curve, a bid-ask cost and a check against real prices first. A search of `tradehub/workshop` and `tradehub/scripts` finds none of these for options. [V] It prices with one flat vol and no spread, the exact case the design warned makes covered calls look too good. Treat all option PASSes (ideas 16, 50) as unproven until calibrated. Options went to live automation first; that order should be reversed.

### 7. The 63-day gate is effectively retired
All 8 active ideas skipped it, with 0 or 1 of 63 days, on Nov 2-3 (or by my pipeline override on Oct 31). [V] Kevin's call, and well recorded. Then be honest about it: show "gate overridden" on cards and the public record, and consider a shorter gate plus a hard size cap.

### 8. Agent gate can be re-rolled
`tradehub/agent/gate.py` always passes `variations=1`, so editing and re-gating resets the bar. [V] Count attempts per spec. Check that a blocked spec can't be edited back to draft (not checked).
